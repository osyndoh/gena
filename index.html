Who We Are

The GENA Initiative develops open-source bioinformatics pipelines, AI-powered analytics, and public health software to advance research, strengthen surveillance, and accelerate evidence-driven responses to infectious disease threats globally.

Vision

To create a world where AI and computational tools drive rapid, data-informed public health decisions, enabling resilient and responsive health systems worldwide.

Mission

To design, implement, and deploy open-source pipelines and AI-driven public health software, making advanced analytics, pathogen genomics, and predictive modeling accessible and actionable for real-world health challenges.
